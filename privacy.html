Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 11, 2026

There are no accounts here and nothing to sign up for. Most of what the site stores is what people deliberately post. Here's the full picture.

What we don't collect

No accounts, no passwords, no email addresses, no payment information, no advertising trackers, and no third-party ad networks. We do not sell or rent any information about you to anyone.

What you post is public

Listings are visible to everyone.

A submitted display includes a street address, map location, an optional display name and description, hours, photos, and an optional submitter name. All of it is public and can be seen by anyone who visits the site. Don't post an address you don't have permission to share, and don't use your legal name in the submitter field if you'd rather stay anonymous.

What we store

  • Listing details you enter: address, latitude and longitude, display or neighborhood name, description, hours, tags, entry notes, and the holiday it belongs to.
  • Photos and video links you upload, stored in our Supabase photo bucket and served publicly.
  • Ratings and comments, including the name you type with them.
  • An edit PIN, if you set one. It's stored server-side, never sent to browsers, and is only used to check that an edit or delete is authorized.
  • A visit counter per listing, which is just a number and isn't tied to any individual.

Location

If you tap the "near me" option, your browser asks your permission and gives the site your approximate coordinates. Those coordinates are used in your browser to sort and filter listings by distance. We do not store or transmit your location to our database. Declining simply means you search by ZIP code instead, and the ZIP you type isn't saved either.

Storage on your device

The site saves your saved favorites in your browser's local storage so they're still there next time. That data stays on your device, is never sent to us, and clearing your browser data removes it. We don't use advertising or tracking cookies.

Services we rely on

  • Google Maps Platform renders maps and handles address lookup. Loading a map sends your IP address and map interactions to Google under Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Supabase hosts the listings database and photo storage.
  • Vercel hosts the site and, like any web host, keeps standard server logs that can include IP addresses and browser type.

Children

The site isn't directed at children under 13 and we don't knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has posted something here, email us and we'll remove it.

Removing your information

If a listing shows your home, or you want a photo, comment, or rating taken down, email hello@myholidaydisplay.com with the address or a link. We'll remove it promptly. You don't need to prove anything beyond a reasonable connection to the property, and we'd rather take down a listing than leave a disputed one up.

California residents

California law gives residents the right to know what personal information a business collects, to request deletion, and not to be discriminated against for exercising those rights. Everything we hold is described above, and you can exercise all of it with a single email to the address in the previous section. We don't sell or share personal information as those terms are defined under the CCPA.

Security

Database access is restricted by row-level security policies, and edit PINs are never exposed to the browser. That said, no site is perfectly secure, and this is a free community project rather than a bank. Please don't post anything here you'd be upset to see made public.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "last updated" date above will change with it. Material changes will be noted on the site.

Contact

Questions about privacy go to hello@myholidaydisplay.com.
